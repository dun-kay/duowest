Our Philosophy.


Duo West is a residential design and engineering practice based in Fremantle/Walyalup.


Founded in 2026, we are inspired by Australian naturalism & European detailing.

We exist for people who already love where they live.


Through thoughtful design, and informed by engineering from the very first sketch.

We create homes that are enduring and built with purpose.

Our Process.


01 · Discover


We understand your home, explore what's possible, discuss budget, planning, heritage and construction options.

We'll leave you with a clear direction for the project and an understanding of what's possible.

  • Concept sketches, layouts & materials.
  • Build, budget & builder advice.
  • A clear roadmap to construction.

From $5,000 / 2 - 4+ weeks



02 · Design


We develop the preferred direction into a considered design; coordinating planning, engineering, and documentation.

We'll take you through the planning approval process, ready for building approval and tender.

  • Drafted plans & council approvals.
  • Engineering & technical coordination.
  • Ready to price, tender, and build.

From $15,000 / 8 - 12+ weeks



03 · Build


We'll help coordinate consultants, builders, and trades, remaining involved as much or as little as you need.

From occasional advice through to full project management, we ensure the finished prodcut reflects the quality of the design.

  • Builders, consultants & construction.
  • Ongoing advice & project management.
  • Support through completion.
